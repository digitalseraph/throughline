Throughline is a job-discovery tool for people who care about the *arc* of their career, not just their next paycheck.

Most job sites give you a flood of postings sorted by recency or keyword overlap. Throughline does something different: it reads your resume the way a thoughtful recruiter would, then for every job it finds it tells you **how well it fits the trajectory you're already on** — and what's missing.

## What you get

For each role in your feed, Throughline shows you:

- A **fit score** from 0–100, generated by Claude reasoning over your resume and the job description.
- A **one-sentence narrative** that frames the role against your career arc — something like *"Natural next step after your platform tenure at Acme; extends ownership without requiring a management pivot."*
- A **prose rationale** (2–4 sentences) explaining why the fit is what it is.
- **Gap chips** color-coded by severity:
  - **Blocker** (red): a hard requirement you don't meet
  - **Stretch** (muted): a nice-to-have you only partially cover
  - **Minor** (outlined): a small gap, probably easy to address

You then move roles you care about into a kanban pipeline (Saved → Applied → Phone screen → Technical → On-site → Offer/Rejected/Withdrawn). Add notes, contacts, and documents per application.

## What Throughline isn't

Throughline doesn't:

- **Auto-apply** to jobs for you. Each application is a deliberate choice.
- **Generate or tailor resumes** per posting. Bring your own resume(s); Throughline reads them.
- **Scrape big-board sites** like Indeed or LinkedIn. We pull from public APIs that ATS providers and job aggregators expose (Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, Adzuna, RemoteOK, Remotive, USAJobs).
- **Hide gaps from you.** If a role wants 7 years of Kubernetes and you've shipped one production cluster, the gap chip says so. We're trying to *help you decide*, not flatter you into applying.

## Who it's for

Throughline is built for people who:

- Have a career that already has a shape (early-career to senior-IC to manager) and want to make their next move *consistent with* that shape.
- Are willing to spend a moment reading the rationale instead of skimming a list.
- Want a single, durable place to track everything from "interesting role" through "follow-up scheduled" through "offer signed (or not)".
